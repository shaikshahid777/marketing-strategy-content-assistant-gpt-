# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Purpose

This brand aims to communicate useful, trustworthy, and audience-focused messages through clear and consistent marketing content.

## Brand Values

- Trust
- Clarity
- Usefulness
- Professionalism
- Audience focus
- Responsible communication

## Brand Voice

The brand voice should be:

- Professional
- Clear
- Helpful
- Confident but not exaggerated
- Friendly and approachable
- Honest and transparent

## Tone

Adapt the tone to the campaign context while maintaining the core brand voice.

### Educational Content
Clear, informative, and helpful.

### Promotional Content
Confident and persuasive without making exaggerated or unsupported claims.

### Social Media Content
Engaging, concise, conversational, and professional.

### Customer-Facing Content
Helpful, respectful, trustworthy, and easy to understand.

## Writing Principles

- Use simple and direct language.
- Focus on audience value.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon.
- Keep messaging consistent with the brand values.
- Use clear calls to action.
- Do not make claims that cannot be verified.
- Avoid misleading or deceptive language.

## Brand Safety

Content must:

- Follow these brand guidelines.
- Avoid unverified claims.
- Avoid misleading promises.
- Avoid discriminatory or hateful language.
- Avoid deceptive marketing practices.
- Avoid unsupported guarantees.
- Avoid potentially harmful or inappropriate messaging.
- Avoid legal or compliance-sensitive claims unless properly verified.

## Prohibited Messaging Patterns

Avoid phrases that:

- Guarantee specific results without evidence.
- Mislead customers about products or services.
- Create unrealistic expectations.
- Make unsupported superiority claims.
- Use manipulative or deceptive language.

## Content Quality Standard

Every generated marketing message should be:

**Clear + Relevant + Brand-consistent + Responsible + Action-oriented**
